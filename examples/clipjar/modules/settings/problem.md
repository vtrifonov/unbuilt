# Settings

A history that grows forever ends up holding years of sensitive text, and one that records everything records what the user never wanted kept. People differ on how much to keep, how long, and from which apps.

Settings give three controls: how many clips and how old, when to stop recording, and which apps to ignore. They also show the paste permission state and say plainly that history is stored unencrypted.

Without them one fixed policy applies, and stricter users leave.
