# History

Captured clips are worthless if they vanish at quit, pile up without limit, or fill with the same sentence copied twenty times. A history also keeps sensitive text on disk, so deleting must mean gone, not hidden.

History keeps earlier copies across restarts, merges repeats and gives honest deletion. It is also where the privacy property users most need to know lives: stored clips are unencrypted and readable by anyone who can read the user's files.

Without it the tool forgets on restart and the list drowns in duplicates.
