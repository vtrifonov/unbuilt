# Picker

A history nobody can reach quickly is no better than none. Digging through a menu of previews, or switching to another window, costs more than copying the text again and pulls the user out of what they were doing.

The picker is a global hotkey, a panel over the current app, a few typed letters and a full preview, all from the keyboard.

Without it the history is a database with no door, and the user falls back to copying again.
