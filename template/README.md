## What it is

<!-- One paragraph: what this is (FORMAT.md §3). -->

my-idea is a small desktop scratch-pad. You press one key, type a short note, and the note is saved and listed with the others.

## Who it is for

<!-- One paragraph: who it is for (FORMAT.md §3). -->

It is for people who want to jot down a thought without opening a document or choosing a folder.

## How to build it

<!-- One paragraph: how to build it, pointing at the /unbuilt:build skill and prompts/build.md in the unbuilt repo (FORMAT.md §3). -->

Run the `/unbuilt:build` skill from the unbuilt plugin on this directory, or paste `prompts/build.md` from the unbuilt repository into your agent. Build the milestones in the order BUILD.md gives them.
